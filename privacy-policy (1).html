Uygulamayı kaldırdığınızda cihazınızdaki tüm veriler silinir. Dilerseniz ilaçlarınızı uygulama içinden tek tek silebilirsiniz.

Privacy Policy – Medicine Tracker

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Data we collect

Medicine Tracker does not collect, transmit to any server, or share any personal data. The medicine names, doses, times, stock counts and “taken” records you enter are stored only on your device. The app requires no account or sign-in.

2. Notifications

Reminders are created locally on your device. No internet connection is required and notification content never leaves your device.

3. Purchases

The Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns, via Google Play, whether the purchase was completed.

4. Ads and tracking

The app shows no ads and does not track users.

5. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device. You can also delete medicines individually inside the app.

6. Medical disclaimer

This app is a reminder tool only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
